# CEUR and SEMANTiCS 2026 Compliance Checklist

This checklist reflects the current manuscript and package in this folder.

## Already addressed

- CEUR-ART LaTeX class is used in `semantics-paper.tex`.
- The PDF is 5 pages including references.
- The first-page copyright clause uses CC BY 4.0 wording.
- The paper is written in English with Latin-script title and metadata.
- The paper has an ethics and environmental-footprint paragraph.
- The paper has a Declaration on Generative AI section.
- The work is framed as a Posters and Demos contribution, not as advertising.
- The Overleaf zip excludes generated build files, local caches, the previous-year award PDFs, and this checklist.

## Must be completed before EasyChair submission

- Replace `SEMANTiCS Project Team` with real natural-person author names.
  CEUR does not allow organizations, teams, or AI programs as authors.
- Add meaningful author affiliations, normally organization and country.
- Add any required author emails and ORCID identifiers if the final template policy requires them.
- Add a public demo URL or a video URL. For demos, the call encourages a video of at most 2 minutes when online inspection is not possible.
- Prepare accessibility metadata for the demo video: captions, transcript, and description.
- Confirm that the submission is original and has not been submitted for publication elsewhere.
- Confirm that at least one author can register for SEMANTiCS 2026 and present in person.
- Review the GenAI declaration against the SEMANTiCS and CEUR policies. The current draft discloses AI-assisted manuscript preparation; human authors should review, edit, and validate all content before submission.

## Proceedings-stage items

These are not part of the individual paper PDF, but they matter after acceptance:

- The proceedings editors collect signed author agreements and editor agreements.
- Final CEUR paper filenames and `index.html` entries are handled by the proceedings editors.
- Accepted posters and demos must follow the final CEUR-WS volume metadata and publication workflow.
